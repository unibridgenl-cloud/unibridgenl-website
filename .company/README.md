# Internal work by the AI agent teams

Drafts, memos, audits and plans produced by the agents in `.claude/agents/`
are saved here, one folder per team (`legal/`, `marketing/`, `audit/`,
`finance/`, `students/`, `sales/`, `tech/`).

This folder is excluded from the public website (`_config.yml`). Never store
real student personal data, passports, grades, contracts or bank records here —
those belong in Google Drive.
